Method and quality

Heatmaps measure texture-dependent brightness variation, not displacement. Sensitivity is a display threshold, not confidence. Isolation masks magnified frames, which can still contain other frequencies. Local amplitudes and phases come from original tracked patches; unsupported areas are not measured. Animation is a fitted operating response, not an identified eigenmode. Verify capture timing, aliasing, exposure blur, rolling shutter, lighting and camera motion.
