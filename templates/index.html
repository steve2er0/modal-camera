Mount controls apply only to the elastic patch, a square with side twice its half-width. The stiffness is dimensionless and represents an assumed transverse restraint. Projection: 0° right, 90° down. Aspect ratio 2 and ν = 0.33 are editable assumptions. One 95 Hz shape cannot uniquely identify geometry or supports.

Guitar strings · geometry & assumptions

Add up to six strings and mark A → B along each. All enabled strings receive simultaneous relative plucks at the same full-string fraction. They vibrate independently; bridge and body coupling are omitted. Each string keeps its own frequency and amplitude scale.

Blank frequency follows the selected response. Enter another string's peak frequency explicitly; tuning is not inferred. At or above camera Nyquist, enter an explicit assumed amplitude too. Full-string fractions 0 and 1 are fixed supports. The guitar preset assumes the visible bridge-to-crop span covers 0–0.5; an image boundary is not automatically a support.

Uniform tension and density, small transverse motion, fixed transverse endpoints. B = 0 gives ideal integer harmonics; B > 0 assumes a pinned stiff-string correction. The selected frequency sets the scale; tension, mass, full length, harmonic index and damping are not identified. Nearby string/body texture can bias patch fits. The body and other strings are omitted. Video compositing reconstructs the narrow background behind the selected string. Large display gains can create artifacts.

Virtual model assumptions

Choose an observed standing shape or generate predicted plate modes. Damping and force scaling are assumed.

Each retained modal coordinate obeys m q̈ + 2ζωm q̇ + mω²q = 0 after a relative impulse. Peak-normalized plate shapes keep their consistent modal masses; these are not recovered kg or N/m matrices. The same point impulse excites all checked modes through their local participation. Single-mode animation is continuous, without a tap or damping envelope. Playback slows all active modes together to show the highest mode at no more than two displayed cycles per second. Higher-mode predictions depend strongly on geometry and mounting and are not additional measurements from the video.

Method and quality

Heatmaps measure texture-dependent brightness variation, not displacement. Sensitivity is a display threshold, not confidence. Isolation masks magnified frames, which can still contain other frequencies. Local amplitudes and phases come from original tracked patches; unsupported areas are not measured. Animation is a fitted operating response, not an identified eigenmode. Verify capture timing, aliasing, exposure blur, rolling shutter, lighting and camera motion.
