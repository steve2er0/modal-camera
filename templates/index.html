Validity, assumptions & recording limits

Use a fixed camera, a constant frame rate and textured surfaces. Motion is a camera-plane projection in source pixels until calibrated. Aliasing, unknown capture/playback speed, exposure blur, compression, changing texture, occlusion and lighting flicker can bias results. Texture, forward/backward error, patch correlation and brightness residual decide which samples are accepted.

The visualization uses linear Eulerian Laplacian-pyramid luminance amplification with an offline zero-phase temporal bandpass. It also amplifies intensity flicker and produces halos for large amplified motions. It is not dense phase-based magnification, an identified structural mode, or a qualified displacement instrument. A filtered operating deflection pattern alone does not identify a mode. No automatic image stabilization is implemented.

Default demos: 60 fps, 6 seconds; P1/P2 Y motion at 3 Hz with peak amplitudes 0.40/0.60 px and P2 leading by 60°, plus 7 Hz at 0.12/0.10 px. The dropout demo occludes P2 from 2.0–3.2 seconds.
